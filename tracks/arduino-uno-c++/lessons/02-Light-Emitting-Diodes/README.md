## Mathematical Proof / Analysis

_Justify the component values and expected behavior._

- **Superconductors:**
    - Superconductors use a disparity between the electrons in the valence band and the conduction band
      in order to conduct (move electrons).
    - There are 3 types of main super conductors, intrinsic, where electrons move via temperature, n-type,
      and p-type. N-type and p-type introduce impurites that either take or give electrons to the valence 
      band or conduction band. 
- **Leds**
    - By placing and n-type next to a p-type and applying a voltage IN THE CORRECT DIRECTION, electrons can
      flow between the two types.
    - In flowing, when an electron and a hole match up the electron can lose energy to fall into the hole. 
      This process is known as Electron Hole Recombination.
    - The change in energy during EHR releases a photon of a wavelength determined by the properties of the material.
